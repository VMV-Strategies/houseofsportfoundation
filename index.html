What We Do & Our Mission

Unlocking Canadian potential through the power of sport.

The House of Sport Foundation bridges the gap between amateur and community sport and sustainable career, educational, and social outcomes across Canada.

What We Do

Connecting grassroots play to real-world pathways.

We design, host, and support athletic leagues, tournaments, and community festivals while building structured pipelines into employment, entrepreneurship, and leadership across sport, recreation, events, and entertainment.

Whether supporting an emerging youth athlete, guiding a student into an industry apprenticeship, or deploying digital innovations that make recreation safer and more accessible, our work ensures every community can thrive.

Our Philosophy

An open Canadian roof where sport meets lifelong opportunity.

We believe sport is one of Canada’s most unifying engines for belonging, resilience, and economic mobility. By bringing together youth, emerging athletes, underserved communities, educators, and employers under one collaborative foundation, we turn athletic participation into lasting personal and professional success.

Governance & Stewardship

Guided by public-benefit stewardship and accountability.

As a Canadian federal not-for-profit organization, The House of Sport Foundation operates strictly on a non-distribution basis—reinvesting 100% of resources into community, youth, and sport initiatives.

Not-for-Profit Commitment

100% Mission-Driven Reinvestment

The House of Sport Foundation operates exclusively for public benefit without private profit. Every grant, sponsorship, and program contribution is reinvested directly into advancing sport accessibility, youth mentorship, workforce readiness, and community infrastructure across Canada.

Non-Distribution Basis

All income and organizational resources are dedicated solely to advancing our community and educational mandate.

Collaborative Stewardship

Working alongside municipalities, Indigenous communities, schools, and industry partners to ensure lasting impact.
